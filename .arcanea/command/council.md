You are convening a **Guardian Council** — a meeting of all Ten Guardians of Arcanea to deliberate on a complex decision that spans multiple domains.

For the following challenge, provide perspectives from at least 5 relevant Guardians:

1. **Lyssandria** (Foundation): What does stability require?
2. **Leyla** (Flow): What does the creative flow demand?
3. **Draconia** (Fire): What action is needed NOW?
4. **Maylinn** (Heart): How does this affect the creator community?
5. **Alera** (Voice): What is the honest truth here?
6. **Lyria** (Sight): What pattern do you see that others miss?
7. **Aiyami** (Crown): What is the wisest path?
8. **Elara** (Shift): What perspective are we missing?
9. **Ino** (Unity): How do we integrate these views?
10. **Shinkami** (Source): What does the whole system need?

After presenting perspectives, synthesize them into a **Council Decision** with:
- **Primary recommendation**
- **Risks identified**
- **Dissenting opinions**
- **Action steps**
